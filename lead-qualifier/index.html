Sales Lead Qualifier

AI Agentic n8n Claude 2026

A proof-of-concept agentic workflow testing one specific assumption: that an agent which decides for itself what to check, and says plainly when it cannot call a lead, earns the right to go unreviewed on everything else. Built end to end in n8n: webhook, two tools the model chooses between, scoring, confidence-based routing, logging, Slack.

Try the demo →
This is a demo with synthetic data. The contact histories and company records are written for this demo. No real people, no real companies, no real pipeline. The point is to test the mechanism, not to represent any actual sales team.
